# Cach su dung AI Project Planner trong Tro ly AI

Planner da duoc gop vao khung Tro ly AI. Nguoi dung khong can mo man hinh planner rieng.

## Quy trinh

1. Cau hinh API key, model va API URL trong AI Settings.
2. Mo Tro ly AI ben phai man hinh.
3. Nhap cau lenh co y dinh lap ke hoach du an.
4. Tro ly AI tao ban nhap ke hoach ngay trong khung chat.
5. Nguoi dung xem danh sach du an, cong viec va nguoi duoc goi y.
6. Bam "Tao du an va cong viec" de tao du lieu that.

## Vi du prompt

```txt
Lap ke hoach du an website ban hang my pham.
Yeu cau gom dang nhap, danh sach san pham, gio hang, thanh toan, trang quan tri,
quan ly don hang va bao cao doanh thu. Hay chia project, task, ky nang can co,
uoc luong gio va goi y nguoi phu hop.
```

## Cum tu kich hoat planner

- "lap ke hoach du an"
- "tao ke hoach du an"
- "chia task"
- "chia viec"
- "phan chia cong viec"

Neu cau lenh khong giong yeu cau lap ke hoach, Tro ly AI van chay che do thao tac giao dien nhu truoc.
